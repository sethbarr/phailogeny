First tournament: debugging agents

The 18 agents of the debugging clade and two controls (no system prompt; a generic expert prompt) each fixed 26 planted bugs, scored by running hidden tests in a sandbox. On Claude Opus 5.5 all 520 patches passed, the no-prompt control included, and every agent cost more than no prompt (median +27%). On Haiku 4.5, 511 of 520 passed at about half the cost, and no agent was reliably better or worse. On 12 harder multi-file bugs, Opus passed 48 of 48 and Haiku 45 of 48.
